Cues

Falling and slow means ease off. Climbing and quick means push. One chord means you're back on target.

You

Zones come from your max heart rate. The default max is 220 minus age, which can be off by 10 or more. Raise it if you see a higher number in a hard effort. The Resting check plan updates your resting heart rate, which sharpens the calorie estimate.

Testing

This app is a training aid, not a medical device. Uneven beat-timing flags are often caused by a dry or shifting strap. Chest pain, fainting, or a racing heart at rest need a doctor, whatever the app shows.
